## Decision and actual usefulness

**The optional native interface works end to end. A quality-preserving Jev advantage has not been demonstrated.** The product angle is an optional typed semantic worker beside `llm` inside Azdaja's persistent context. Keep exact work in code, retain sources and distributions, and let the caller choose further reasoning. Do not turn a model's confidence into a compulsory gate or discard competing evidence.

The newest real comparison returned **17/18 correct with Jev versus 18/18 with the matched generative selector**. Median observed block latency was 1.162s versus 2.529s, but confusing ambiguity with absence failed the predeclared no-quality-loss and benefit bars. No post-hoc threshold, repair call or rerun was used. The earlier seven-task answer study also showed no final-answer gain. These are small authored development panels, not representative efficacy estimates.

See the [concise angle, control flow and seven application directions](jev-angle-of-attack-20260917.md), [real source-selection outputs](../../bench/jev/span_selection/RESULTS.md), and [primary-source research](jev-primary-evidence-20260916.md). Retrieval, exact extraction, joins, memory conflicts, ETL and adaptive orchestration are separate applications. Native availability does not establish an automatic planner, an RLM-specific advantage, or a moat over the same models with ordinary Python loops and cache.
