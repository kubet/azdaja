## Shared errors, calibration and disagreement

Keep all official labels unchanged. Record exact five shared messages, provenance,
ordinary textual interpretation and missing context. Agent review is not an
independent human adjudication and cannot establish label noise by itself.

Compute Brier and binary positive-probability ECE using both fixed 5 and 10
equal-width bins, inclusive endpoint 1 in the final bin. These are descriptive
fixed-panel diagnostics with duplicate/dependence caveats, not calibrated safety
certificates. Sum(p)-gold does not identify overconfidence on each individual FP.

Agreement/disagreement policy is fixed before new adjudication: preserve both
original decisions; disagreements escalate, agreements retain the GENERATIVE
label as an existing baseline decision, not a Jev approval. Report shared-error
floor, coverage, marginal calls and an explicitly hypothetical perfect-adjudicator
ceiling. A separately frozen actual adjudication may inspect only raw four
messages and unchanged task, with no prior answers or gold. At most one generative
adjudication call. No claim that an extra model pass is free.
